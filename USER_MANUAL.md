# UNIFIX user manual

Choose the guide for your role:

- [Public reporter](docs/user-manual-public.md)
- [Employee/requester](docs/user-manual-employee.md)
- [Assistant/reviewer](docs/user-manual-assistant.md)
- [Maintenance personnel](docs/user-manual-personnel.md)
- [Administrator](docs/user-manual-admin.md)

Public reporters can submit and track reports without signing in. Employees,
Assistants, Personnel, and Admins sign in with their university account. Admins
and Assistants share application permissions; the database keeps their roles
distinct for first-Admin setup and last-Admin safeguards.
