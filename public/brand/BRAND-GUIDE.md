# UNIFIX brand guide

## Brand idea

UNIFIX brings university and campus care together. The shield represents a trusted, protected campus; the simple campus facade identifies the place we serve; and the gold checked badge marks a resolved issue. The mark is an original UNIFIX symbol and does not use the NVSU seal.

## Logo files

- `unifix-logo-horizontal-light.svg` — primary horizontal logo for light backgrounds.
- `unifix-logo-horizontal-dark.svg` — horizontal reverse logo for dark backgrounds.
- `unifix-logo-stacked-light.svg` and `unifix-logo-stacked-dark.svg` — centered stack variants.
- `unifix-icon-square.svg` — rounded-square app icon.
- `unifix-icon-circle.svg` — circular avatar/icon crop.
- `unifix-icon-green.svg`, `unifix-icon-white.svg`, `unifix-icon-black.svg` — single-color marks for one-ink applications.

The logo lettering is live SVG text. Use Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold when available; Poppins ExtraBold, Montserrat ExtraBold, or Arial Bold are fallbacks. Interface wordmarks should use the system's existing Plus Jakarta Sans font.

## Clear space and size

Keep at least one-quarter of the shield width clear on every side of the complete logo. Do not place text, borders, or imagery inside this space. Use the horizontal logo no smaller than 188 px wide and the stacked logo no smaller than 120 px tall. Use the icon-only mark at 24 px or larger; for smaller sizes, use a simplified app/favicon crop and preserve the silhouette.

## Usage

Choose the light variant on white or cream and the dark variant on deep green or other dark colors. Keep the mark flat and at its original proportions. Do not recolor the check independently in monochrome versions, add effects, stretch, rotate, or place the full-color logo over busy imagery.
